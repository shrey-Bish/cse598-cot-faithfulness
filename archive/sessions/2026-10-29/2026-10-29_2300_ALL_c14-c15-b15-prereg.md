## 3. C14 (exploratory, no calls): two reviews that disagree, as the escalation signal

**Code:** `agents/disagreement.py`. Summary in `results/trackC/c14_disagreement.json` (numbers only; exported to
`results/summaries/trackC/`).

**Data:** C4's 159 tool-team cases (53 items, 75 bad lookups and 84 good, 3 repeats), as reviewed in C4, C9, C10, C11
and C12.

**The rule** (fixed here and frozen for C15):
- Escalate when the answer_only review and the same reviewer model's trace-seeing review (+ private reasoning, or
  + tool log) give different letters, or either gives no letter.
- Otherwise ship the letter both gave.

**What it's compared against:** the checker's pipeline with the same trace-seeing reviewer (C4's rule: a flag
escalates, otherwise that review's letter ships). The comparison is paired by item, with a 95% bootstrap clustered
by item.

**The two pairings C15 repeats** (C4's prompt; + tool log):

| reviewer | bad: escalated (checker flags 73 of 75) | caught | checker pipeline | caught − checker pipeline | shipped wrong | good: false alarms | checker pipeline | false alarms − checker pipeline | tokens per case: added review / checker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Olmo 3.1 32B Instruct | 67 of 75 | 69 of 73 | 73 of 73 | −5 [−14, +0] | 4 of 73 | 17 of 84 | 7 of 84 | **+12 [+1, +24]** | 1,877 / 4,786 |
| Qwen3 30B Instruct | 24 of 75 | 73 of 73 | 73 of 73 | +0 [+0, +0] | 0 of 73 | 13 of 84 | 12 of 84 | +1 [−6, +8] | 1,416 / 4,786 |

**Across all 20 pairings** (5 logs × 2 kinds of trace × 2 reviewers):
- **Catches:** the checker's pipeline catches 73 of 73 every time. The rule catches 56–73 of 73. It is never ahead
  (caught − checker pipeline: 0 to −23).
- **The signal alone is weaker:** escalation is −8 to −88 points below the checker's flag in every pairing. When
  both reviews agree on the right letter, the rule ships it instead of escalating. That is a catch, but not a flag.
- **False alarms:** with the Olmo reviewers, which defer, the rule raises 12–21 points more than the checker's
  pipeline. Olmo 3 7B (C11) is the worst: +21 [+7, +36], and it catches only 56 of 73. With the Qwen3 reviewers,
  which don't defer, it is −2 to +6, with intervals that include 0.
- **Cost:** the added answer_only review takes 1,400–2,050 tokens per case with the instruct reviewers, 3,639 with
  Qwen3 Thinking. The checker's re-ask takes 4,786. So the rule costs about 30–45% of the checker's tokens.
- **By set:** the Olmo 32B reviewer's extra false alarms are on MMLU-Pro (good lookups escalated: 15 of 57, against
  2 of 27 on the puzzles).

**Reading (exploratory; these cases chose the rule, so nothing here is a finding):**
- Disagreement is not a better signal than the checker.
- With a reviewer that doesn't defer, it matched the checker's pipeline here, at about a third of the tokens.
- With a reviewer that defers, it ships a few wrong answers (both reviews agree on the wrong letter) and adds false
  alarms (the blind review misses right answers on MMLU-Pro).
- C15 tests the two claims (c1, c2 below) on fresh items.

## 8. B1's alpha rule is fixed in advance (checked)

- **Where the rule is:** `finetune/gpu/provenance_job.py`'s docstring ("The rule for alpha (written Oct 29, before
  any set A number exists)") and `choose_alpha`, from commit `0bd2241` (Oct 29 00:31).
- **Before any data:** that was before set A's runs existed (09:32) and before the forced job was queued (21:46).
- **On GitHub, tested:** it has been on GitHub since the morning push, and it is tested
  (`test_forced_gives_each_alpha_and_span_and_chooses_by_the_rule`).
- **The rule:**
  - At cut (ii), per span, against alpha = 1: dW, dO and dC (P of the wrong letter; P of the own answer; P of the
    own answer with a right-letter hint).
  - **Eligible:** dO ≥ 0 and dC ≥ −0.05.
  - **Chosen:** the eligible alpha with the most negative dW; within 0.01 of it, the larger alpha.
  - **None eligible:** alpha = 1.
  - It is chosen only on set A, never on the 30 evaluation questions.
