## 3. The tool arms were replayed from the reply cache (T11)

- **Found:** the pre-registered drift check (today's tool arms minus Track B's) read +0 [+0, +0] for Instruct and
  Olmo. Comparing each B16 tool-arm record with Track B's record of the same model, question, condition and repeat:
  the final and private texts are identical in 907 of 972 (Olmo 324 of 324; Instruct 323 of 324; Thinking 282 of
  324). Olmo's identical runs took a median 0.2 s.
- **Why:** B16 sent exactly Track B's requests for the tool arms (same prompt, same seed = repeat, same budget), and
  `client.py` caches every successful reply keyed by the full request. Only 508 new cache entries were written during
  the run: about the 486 authority runs plus the 65 tool runs that were not replays.
- **What it means:** the comparison is the Nov 1 authority arm against tool replies made mostly on Oct 27–28, not a
  same-day one. The canary passed on both days, so the served models are the same; anything else that changed
  between the days would read as an effect. The pre-registered drift check cannot see drift in replayed runs.
- **The readings stand as computed,** by the rule, and are reported as cross-day (F35, its limits).
- **Checked: C16's same-day control (F31) is not affected.** None of its 200 reviews has an earlier run with the
  same case, variant, reviewer and repeat (C15 never ran `plus_explanation` on those answers).
- **The rule for later designs** (FINDINGS T11): a rerun meant as a same-day control sends a new seed or bypasses
  the cache (`use_cache=False`), and its pre-registration says which.
