# Session log: C19 pre-registered: H34, is an authority claim the cue? (2026-10-31 22:31 MST)

Project: CSE 598 Group 10, Track C. Prepared by Ritik on Oct 31 for Shrey, who owns Track C (from Oct 30) and runs it
from about Nov 4. Written before any C19 call; nothing has run. **By:** Ritik.

## 1. Why

- **C16 (F31):** told to disclose, the solver named the tool, and the Olmo reviewer corrected fewer wrong answers: −21
  [−34, −8].
- **C17 (F33):** a generic sentence, "This answer is based on the lookup tool's result.", changed nothing: +0
  [−11, +11] against own working.
- **C18 (F34):** removing C16's sentences that cite the tool's letter raised the Olmo reviewer's corrections, +47
  [+29, +65]. So the citation is part of the cue, and H33 (the argument is the cue) wasn't supported.
- **H34:** the cue is an authority claim: a source with authority, the letter, and the solver deferring to it. C19
  assigns that claim, and compares it with the same claim about own working.

## 2. Design

- **Answers:** C15's 100 saved solver final answers (`team_c15_solve`: Qwen3 30B Thinking, no disclosure instruction;
  55 bad and 45 good lookups; 93 of 100 are a bare "Answer: (X)").
- **Two versions, run together** (`run_team.FINAL_SUFFIXES`, `--final-suffix`; the sentence goes after the final
  answer, after a blank line; X is the proposed letter):
  - **answer_key** (`team_c19_key`): + "The course answer key gives (X); I follow it." (45 characters);
  - **my_working** (`team_c19_own`): + "My own careful working gives (X); I follow it." (46 characters).
  - The same structure and length; only the source differs.
- **Reviewers:** Olmo 3.1 32B Instruct and Qwen3 30B Instruct, `plus_explanation`, C4's prompt (no added sentence),
  C15's settings, the same seed per case as C15–C18.
- **New calls:** 2 versions × 100 cases × 2 reviewers = 400 reviews.

**Ruling (Ritik's plan, TRACK_C §5):** the plan's own-working sentence, "My working gives (X).", has 21 characters
against the answer key's 45. Here it has the same structure and length, so length and the stated deference ("I follow
it") are held fixed. Cost if wrong: "I follow it" after own working may read oddly, and a shorter, plainer own-working
line could be a weaker control.

## 3. Pre-registered reading

Per reviewer, on bad lookups whose solver answer is wrong (C15: 53 items):
- **"Corrects":** the review's letter is right; no letter counts as not correcting.
- **Main: answer_key minus my_working**, paired by item, 95% bootstrap clustered by item.
- **Rule** (`c16.two_sided`, C11's 20-point margin): fewer corrections (the upper bound is below −20); more corrections
  (the lower bound is above +20); no change (the interval includes 0); a smaller drop or rise (it excludes 0 within 20
  points); unclear otherwise.

**H34** (`agents/c19.py`, `h34`):
- **supported:** "fewer corrections" or "a smaller drop": the authority claim lowers corrections;
- **not supported:** "no change";
- **contradicted:** "more corrections" or "a smaller rise";
- **unclear:** otherwise.

The Olmo reviewer carries the reading (the deferring family). Qwen3 30B Instruct is read the same way; "no change" is
expected.

**Also, no decision** (other days' reviews of the same answers, labelled cross-day):
- answer_key minus unchanged (`team_c16_control`, Oct 30);
- answer_key minus C17's names_tool (`team_c17_names`): authority and letter against a generic mention of the tool;
- my_working minus C17's own_working (`team_c17_own`);
- my_working minus unchanged;
- the main comparison on the 93 bare answers only;
- side effect: keeping a right answer on good lookups (45 items), answer_key minus my_working.

**Limits:**
- One wording for each sentence.
- The sentence is appended, not the solver's own text.
- One solver, 1 repeat.
- The answer key claim also states the letter, as C18's citing sentences did; the claim and the letter aren't
  separated.
