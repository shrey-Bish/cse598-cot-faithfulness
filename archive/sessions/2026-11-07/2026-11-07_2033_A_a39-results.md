## 4. What it means, as A39's log said in advance (§4)

> **Something "fewer than the instruction":** the first fix that beats one line. **A combination "adds to both":**
> they stack. **The wrapper "works":** marking the source at the system level does what training didn't.
> **Everything "no difference detected" at ±5:** the instruction is as good as these fixes get, now with the power to
> say so.

- **"Fewer than the instruction" (R2): LoRA + note,** −8 [−12, −4]. In §4's words, the first fix that beats one line,
  on the one measure R2 reads (following). R2 reports acceptance beside it without reading it: −9 [−13, −5]. R3 reads
  the same system "adds, costs acceptance". wrapper + note's R2 "fewer" is diluted by design; its informative reading
  is R3's "adds, costs acceptance".
- **"Works" (R4): not reached.** The wrapper alone reads "no effect": on E2, marking the source at the system level
  does not, alone, do what training didn't.
- **"Adds to both" (R3): LoRA + wrapper,** on the inserted tool turn: they stack. wrapper + note and LoRA + note
  "add, costs acceptance".
- **Not everything is "no difference detected",** so §4's last meaning doesn't apply.
