## 4. What it means

- **B1's verdict on the 30 stays the primary result** (§5, §13.7, §21). B1R2 is **a labelled replication on E2**,
  reported as it came out.
- **The cut in following replicates.** On E2's 120 questions, scaling attention to the inserted tool turn cuts
  following of a wrong tool hint by 13.8 points [−20.0, −7.5], an interval well below 0. On the 30 it was −17
  [−30, −3]. As a share of alpha = 1's following, 40% fewer (82 → 49) against the 30's 38% (26 → 16); described, not
  read.
- **Acceptance still falls past the 5-point limit,** so the rule's word is the same: "ignores tools", not "works".
  The fall is 5.8 points (211 → 197 of 240: 14 runs, where 5 points is 12).
  - **What E2 adds:** the acceptance interval, [−10.8, −1.3], now excludes 0. The cost to a correct tool hint is
    real, which the 30 couldn't show ([−20, +7]).
  - **What it doesn't settle:** that interval includes −5, so whether the cost exceeds the limit isn't resolved. The
    word rests on a point estimate 0.8 points (2 runs of 240) past the limit, as the rule reads it.
- **No-hint accuracy doesn't move** (+0.8 [−6.7, +8.3]). A no-hint prompt has no tool turn, so the scaling has no
  key to act on. That fits F38's account of the 30's +10 as sampling variation between jobs.
- **The intervals are as wide as planned:** half-widths ±4.8 (acceptance) and ±6.3 (following). B1R's power check
  gave ±4.7 to ±6.7 and ±6.7 at 120 questions (the A39 log's §2).
- **In plain terms:** the fix cuts wrong-hint following by about 14 points on 120 new questions, as it did on the 30,
  and costs about 6 points of correct tool hints taken. The cost is now clearly above 0, and by B1's rule it is just
  over the limit.

## 5. What limits the reading

- **One model** (Olmo 3 7B Think, base, the fixed Hugging Face engine `-v2`), one alpha (set A's 0.25, not
  re-chosen), the deployable span only (no oracle span on E2).
- **E2's categories:** law, engineering, chemistry and physics, 30 each, 10-option MMLU-Pro questions used nowhere
  else (the A39 log's §2). They are other questions than the 30, with other base rates at alpha = 1 (following 34%
  against 43%, acceptance 88% against 83%, no-hint accuracy 65% against 53%), so the two studies stand side by side
  and are never pooled.
- **The inserted tool turn only:** the pasted block, the user's hint and a real tool call weren't run.
- **2 runs a hint a question** (2 wrong-hint, 2 correct-hint, 1 no-hint); every interval is clustered by question.
- **The rule's 5-point limits read point estimates:** the acceptance word rests on −5.8 against −5, with an interval
  that includes −5.
- **No answer** (counted as not following and not taking, and counted): alpha = 1, 9 of 600 (5 of 240 wrong-hint, 2 of
  240 correct-hint, 2 of 120 no-hint); alpha 0.25, 7 of 600 (4, 1 and 2). No imbalance.
