## 1. Why

- **F39 (A18):** inside the fixed model, LoRA "acts in the reasoning". On base's own reasoning, LoRA's weights pull
  toward the hint's letter harder, not less: R_LoRA −0.72 logits [−1.02, −0.39], with the extra pull coming with
  layers 24–31. What LoRA changed is the reasoning it writes.
- **A40 (bias-augmented consistency training)** pairs a hinted prompt with reasoning written without the hint and an
  answer that follows that reasoning (A40's log, §1–§3). If that teaches the weights to discount the prompt's hint
  when they answer, it shows on base's own tokens as a reduction of the hint's direct effect (R above 0), which LoRA
  didn't show.
- **The question** (Ritik's, Nov 6): does consistency training change the readout at layers 15, 19 and 23, as A18
  asked of LoRA? And whatever A40 reads on E2, A41 says where its weights act.

## 3. The rules, fixed now

95% bootstraps clustered by question (20,000 resamples, seed 0); a case counts where TE_base is at least 0.5
(patching's MIN_GAP); fewer than 5 questions is "too few".

**Primary, A18's rule with A40 in LoRA's place** (tool-hint cases):
1. **Does A40 act on these tokens?** R_A40's interval lies above 0. If not: **"acts in the reasoning"** (the layer
   split reported, not read).
2. **If it does, where:** s(S) = ΣR(base with A40 on S) / ΣR_A40, a ratio of sums resampled by question.
   - **"stops the readout":** s(0–23)'s lower bound is at least 0.5 and s(24–31)'s upper bound below 0.5. Then
     **"at the readout layers"** if s(15, 19, 23)'s lower bound is at least 0.5, else **"before them"** if s(0–14)'s
     is, else **"spread over 0–23"**.
   - **"acts after the readout":** s(24–31)'s lower bound is at least 0.5 and s(0–23)'s upper bound below 0.5.
   - **"either half suffices":** both lower bounds at least 0.5.
   - **"unclear":** anything else.

**Secondary, A40 against LoRA on the same cases** (the comparison A41 exists for):
- Per case: R_A40 − R_LoRA. Each R is taken against its own job's TE_base: LoRA's from A18's swap rows
  (`$TRACKA_OUT/a18/swap/swap_rows.jsonl`), A40's from A41's.
- A case counts where both jobs saw TE_base of at least 0.5. Cases are matched by A18's job id. The mean is
  clustered by question.
- **"A40 removes more of the hint's pull than LoRA"** (interval above 0), **"A40 removes less than LoRA"** (below 0),
  or **"no difference detected"**.
- Reported beside it: the largest drift of TE_base between the two jobs (the same tokens and the same base weights,
  so it should be near 0), and any case with no LoRA row.

**Reported, no decision:**
- each swap's plain R (base's effect minus the hybrid's). A18 showed this after the fact; it is fixed here in advance;
- the share of base's effect removed;
- how often A40's forced answer at base's cut is still the hint's letter (X of N);
- F12's heads under A40 minus base on the same tokens;
- Step 10's share at the readout under A40's weights on base's cases, beside base's 0.97 [0.85, 1.11] and LoRA's
  1.01 [0.85, 1.23] (F39).

**User-hint cases:** the same readings and numbers, reported apart under "secondary: user hints", never pooled.

## 4. What each outcome means, said now

- **Stops the readout, at the readout layers:** BCT changes how the hint's letter is read off base's reasoning at
  the three layers F11 found. LoRA had no such mechanism.
- **Acts after the readout:** BCT discounts the letter after the readout, in layers 24–31, where LoRA added to the
  pull.
- **Either half suffices, or unclear:** A40 acts on these tokens, and its place isn't resolved.
- **Acts in the reasoning:** like LoRA, BCT changes what the model writes, not how it reads the hint off base's
  reasoning. Any gain on E2 comes through the text.
- **Against LoRA:**
  - *more:* the kind of target (unhinted reasoning against hinted) changes how the weights read the hint;
  - *less:* A40's weights pull toward the hint more than LoRA's did;
  - *no difference:* at this size, the kind of target doesn't change the read-off.
- **None of these changes A40's E2 reading** (its own rule, read Nov 17) or F39.
