## 2. The primary: the swap on base's tool-hint cases (§4.1–2)

- **Cases:** 30 at the takeover; 28 with base's effect at least 0.5 (2 left out); 13 questions.
- **R_LoRA** = TE_base − TE_LoRA, what LoRA's weights remove of the hint's direct effect on base's tokens:
  **−0.72 logits [−1.02, −0.39]** (95% bootstrap clustered by question, 20,000 resamples, seed 0).
- **The rule:** R_LoRA's interval must lie above 0 for training to act on these tokens. It lies below 0. **Reading:
  "acts in the reasoning".** The layer split is reported, not read.
- **In plain words:** give the trained weights the reasoning base wrote, and the hint pulls the answer toward its letter
  **harder**, not less: about a quarter more (the share of base's effect removed is −23% [−36%, −9%]). Under LoRA's
  weights, the forced answer at base's cut is still the hint's letter in **30 of 30** cases (base: 30 of 30). So
  training didn't cut the readout, or discount the letter after it. What changed is the reasoning LoRA writes before
  the answer.
- **The layer split (reported, not read).** The rule's shares, s(S) = ΣR(base with LoRA on S) / ΣR(LoRA), are
  undefined here: ΣR(LoRA) is below 0 in every one of the 20,000 resamples. So the plain R of each swap is shown
  instead, with the same bootstrap. That presentation was chosen after seeing this; nothing is read from it.

  | weights | R (logits) |

  | base + LoRA's layers 0–14 | +0.07 [+0.01, +0.15] |
