# 02. The numbers

Every number a slide or the report might use, as frozen (the tag `results-freeze-2026-11-18`), with its finding and its numbers-only source. Wording and numbers are FINDINGS' ([`docs/FINDINGS.md`](../../FINDINGS.md), with [`FINDINGS_B.md`](../../trackB/FINDINGS_B.md) and [`FINDINGS_C.md`](../../trackC/FINDINGS_C.md)); "STORY" is FINDINGS' summary at its top. Three rows of the glance table are in FINDINGS' own words here (F4, FC2, FC4: the same numbers).

- **How to read:** rates are "k of n" runs (or items) with a 95% Wilson interval where one is given; differences are percentage points with a 95% bootstrap clustered by question (by item in Track C). "Exploratory" marks a result FINDINGS marks post hoc, after the fact, descriptive or not pre-registered.
- **Checked:** `cot-disclosure/tests/test_presentation_pack.py` finds every "k of n" and interval of each row in its finding's section of FINDINGS (or the STORY), every source file in the repo, and every "k of n" as a k and n pair, and every interval bound, in the row's JSON source. Rows whose source is per-run (`.jsonl`) are checked against FINDINGS only.
- **Plain names:** the codes in parentheses are for searching FINDINGS; [`docs/GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md) gives each one's plain name.
