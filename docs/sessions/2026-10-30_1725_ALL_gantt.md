# Session log: the Gantt sheet, generated from the repo (2026-10-30 17:25 MST)

Project: CSE 598 Group 10, all tracks. Ritik asked for a project Gantt that can be refreshed any day from the repo's
own sources. **By:** Ritik.

## 1. What it is

`tools/make_gantt.py` (from `cot-disclosure/`: `../.venv/bin/python tools/make_gantt.py`; `--today`, `--dry-run`). It
reads `git log`, the session logs and their INDEX, the dailies, TODO, PLAN and FINDINGS, and writes:

- **`docs/progress/GANTT.xlsx`**, five sheets:
  - **Gantt:** every task by track and phase, a column per day from Oct 1 to Dec 12, filled by status (done, in
    progress, planned, blocked), with milestones and a today column;
  - **Daily:** per day and track, the work, the runs, the findings and the commits;
  - **Changes:** every commit;
  - **Progress:** per track, done of total, the percent by time, and the hours left;
  - **Remaining:** every open task with owner, start, due date, estimate, dependency and status.
- **`docs/progress/gantt.png`:** one page for slides.
- **`docs/progress/STATUS.md`:** percent done per track, the next 7 days, milestones.

A date taken from a commit before the session logs began (Oct 27) is marked "inferred": 36 task dates and 16 Daily
rows. Tested on a fake history in a temp git repo (36 tests).
