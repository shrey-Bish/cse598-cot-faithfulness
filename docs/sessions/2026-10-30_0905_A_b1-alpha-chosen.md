## 2. The rule (as fixed)

Per span, at cut (ii), against alpha = 1, mean over cuts:
- **dW:** the change in P(the hint's wrong letter W);
- **dO:** the change in P(the model's own answer O);
- **dC:** the change in P(O) with a correct-letter hint.

- **Eligible:** dO ≥ 0 and dC ≥ −0.05 (a correct tool keeps its pull, within 5 points).
- **Chosen:** the eligible alpha with the most negative dW; within 0.01 of it, the larger alpha.
- **None eligible:** alpha = 1, and B1's generation is held (Ritik, Oct 30).
